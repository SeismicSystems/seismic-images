Summary for NVMe Persistent Disks:
==================================
1. Core NVMe support appears intact (base NVMe driver not shown = likely still enabled)
2. NVMe over network protocols (TCP, FC) are ENABLED
3. Device mapper crypto is ENABLED - critical for encrypted persistent disks
4. VirtIO drivers are ENABLED - important for GCP virtualization
5. Hardware crypto acceleration (AES-NI) is ENABLED
6. Block device RAM disk support is optimized for minimal usage
7. Most legacy storage controllers and protocols are DISABLED

Key concerns for persistent disk functionality:
- NVMe hardware monitoring is disabled (may affect diagnostics)
- Block I/O tracing is disabled (may affect debugging)
- Device mapper uevents are disabled (may affect auto-mounting)
- Filesystem encryption is disabled (relies on device-level encryption)
